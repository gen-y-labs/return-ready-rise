# Return Ready Rise

**Find where your experience fits, and prepare your next application.**

## The problem
Many people step away from paid work for a while: to raise children, care for a parent or partner, or handle other life responsibilities. Coming back is harder than it should be. People are unsure which jobs their experience still fits, how to explain the gap, and whether a job's hours will work with their life. A career gap is often read as a weakness, even when the person brings years of real experience.

## Our purpose
Make a career break a normal part of a work history, and help people return with confidence and an honest, strong application.

## What we built
Return Ready Rise is an AI assistant for anyone returning after a career break. A person types or speaks their story, and the app:
- Turns their past work and break-time activities into skills, each tied to their own words
- Shows which job areas fit them, with a clear score and the reasons behind it
- Lists jobs that match their hours and location, and flags schedule conflicts
- Builds a step-by-step plan to close the gaps for a chosen job
- Writes a one-page résumé that shows the break as a normal, dated entry, with nothing invented

## Who benefits
Anyone returning to work after a break, of any gender, age, background, or field. The career gap is the only thing they need in common, whatever the reason for it or the work they did before. Employers benefit too, by seeing experienced people they might otherwise overlook.
